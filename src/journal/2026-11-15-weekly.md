*Spooky Baron's What's New · week of 9 to 15 November 2026*

The site grew up this week. It swapped beige for a dark theme with a lime accent, picked up a big landing page and new Videos and Social pages, and on Wednesday moved to its own address, spookybaron.co.uk. The old address still works. The Social page now has scrolling YouTube rows like the TikTok feed, and new uploads turn up on their own.

The games got some love too. In Eddie vs Richie, Richie no longer clobbers himself with his own thrown kettles, and Eddie can finally pick things up and throw them. BuffWatch, a small World of Warcraft add-on that flashes a big warning when one of your own buffs wears off, is waiting for an in-game test. There’s also an idea brewing for a Curse of Monkey Island-style adventure starring Eddie and Richie. The verdict on the current sprites was “pretty pants”, so a new art style is on order first.

On the video side, Speakerphone Steve is a 30-second stick-figure comedy about people who walk down the street on loudspeaker calls, with a pizza-slice phone and some very public verruca questions. Helpdesk Horde got a 44-second advert: blue screens, URGENT stamps and “have you tried turning it off and on again”. Three older videos got fresh thumbnails and titles as well.

Four Shorts went public: a raid clip, “I’ll Be Watching You” from GTA Crew Madness, and a snowy WoW clip that went up overnight as “Beautiful... Then a Classic Orc Runs Past”. There was no orc. It was a dwarf. The title has been fixed, and from now on Luke gets the final say before anything goes public.

The fourth was the week’s hit, “What If You Fell Off Blackpool Tower?”: 45 seconds, a height counter ticking down, real fall physics and a seagull that nicks your chips on the way down. More What Ifs are in the pipeline.

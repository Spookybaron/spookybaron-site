*Spooky Baron's What's New · Thursday 29 October 2026*

A busy first day of making things in public. Two puppets, Tango and Skye, got voices of their own, and a short called “The Spare Arm” started to take shape. Another set of frames, “Oxford Is Older Than the Aztecs”, was cut in the same bright, simple style.

The other new toy was a game. Grok Snake is a neon arcade snake: rainbow colours, a bit of speed, and the usual rule about not biting your own tail. Luke had a first playable version by the end of the day.

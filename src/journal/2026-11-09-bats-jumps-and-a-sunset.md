*Spooky Baron's What's New · Monday 9 November 2026*

Monday belonged to the games. Cheeky Pete and Grok Snake had a tidy-up: small clean-ups only, and the play itself did not change. One Snake quirk was spotted and left for later. An arrow key on the game-over screen starts a new run straight away, so a key you are already mashing can throw the results away.

Eddie vs Richie got the bigger pass. A proper title screen, best of three rounds, a real bat swing, jumping, and a theme tune. It feels less like a test and more like a bout.

In the evening a cartoon called “Speakerphone Steve” was drawn and given a voice. A small one, but it is in the same pile as the other shorts.

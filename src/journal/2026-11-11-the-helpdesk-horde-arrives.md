*Spooky Baron's What's New · Wednesday 11 November 2026*

A fourth game joined the others overnight. Helpdesk Horde is a survivors-style scramble: a crowd of problems heads your way, you fire off replies, and you try to last until home time. Closed tickets level you up. Each level lets you pick a skill, from a mail-merge trick to the oldest fix in the book, turning it off and on again.

By breakfast it had a cast, recorded voices and a trailer, and it is playable on the site next to Eddie vs Richie, Grok Snake and Cheeky Pete. The game cards also picked up faded screenshots, so you can see a scrap of each game before you press play. This log was brought up to date at the same time.

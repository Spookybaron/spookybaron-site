*Spooky Baron's What's New · Saturday 7 November 2026*

A softer Saturday, spent looking at how a stick-figure film was put together. The pictures are simple: a farmer and an ox, a wooden ship, a desk and a manuscript. The movement is the interesting bit. The legs, the curtains and a hand with a candle all shift like short clips of animation, not like drawings tweened in code.
